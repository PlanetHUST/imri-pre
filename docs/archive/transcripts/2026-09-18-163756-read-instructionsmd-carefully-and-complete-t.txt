  A merger-tree → single-halo execution bridge, in five new modules plus two

  What the calibration established

  1. The threshold is a gap-depth statement, not a bare number. The project's
  own Type-I gap denominator 1 + K/κ_norm with κ_norm = 25 is algebraically 
  identical to the Kanagawa gap-depth law:

  Σ_gap / Σ₀ = 1 / (1 + 0.04 K)     — verified to 2.2×10⁻¹⁶

  So K > K* means "the gap is cleared below residual surface density f = 
  1/(1+0.04K*)". That's the real calibration win: the threshold now has units of
  meaning. depletion_fraction() and threshold_for_depletion() convert both
  ways.

  2. K = 25 is the one value the model fixes by itself — the half-depth point (f
  = 0.5), the same κ_norm the Type-I torque already uses. Every other value in
  the family is chosen; this one is determined.

  3. I tested the obvious alternative and rejected it. One could place the
  switch where |Γ_I| = |Γ_II|, making the transition continuous. Measured across
  the SMBH × migrator grid, that crossover spans a factor of 2.5×10⁶ in K (26
  at 10⁶ M☉/10 M☉ → 6.4×10⁷ at 10³ M☉/100 M☉). No single global threshold can 
  make the switch continuous. The discontinuity is irreducible for any global-K
  criterion — worth knowing, since it rules out a whole line of attack.
