# Figure captions

Migration scenario: semi-analytic without traps.

## AGN-assisted transport through one active episode

Cumulative gas-transport completion rate for systems eligible for downstream evolution under the semi-analytic without traps migration scenario during a 10 Myr AGN episode at redshift $z=10$ for an NSC mass of $2\times10^{5}\,M_\odot$. Curves distinguish SMBH mass and summarize 2 Monte Carlo repeats. Gas completion is not physical LISA entry. This is an exploratory result with fewer than 10 valid repeats; no uncertainty band is shown.

## Physical LISA-entry IMRI production

Cumulative physical LISA-entry rate versus entry time under the semi-analytic without traps migration scenario at redshift $z=10$. The time coordinate is the persisted `detection_time_myr`, and the curve may extend beyond the AGN duty cycle. Rates retain the 10 Myr AGN-episode denominator. This is an exploratory result with fewer than 10 valid repeats; no uncertainty band is shown.

## Physical LISA-entry IMRI rate versus redshift

Final intrinsic physical LISA-entry IMRI rate under the semi-analytic without traps migration scenario as a function of redshift for an NSC mass of $2\times10^{5}\,M_\odot$. Colors distinguish SMBH mass; each bin uses 2 Monte Carlo repeats and a 10 Myr AGN-duty rate denominator, including entries that occur later. This is an exploratory result with fewer than 10 valid repeats; no uncertainty band is shown. A cross marks an unavailable result, not zero.

## Physical LISA-entry IMRI rate versus SMBH mass

Final intrinsic physical LISA-entry IMRI rate under the semi-analytic without traps migration scenario as a function of SMBH mass for an NSC mass of $2\times10^{5}\,M_\odot$. Colors distinguish redshift; each bin uses 2 Monte Carlo repeats and a 10 Myr AGN-duty rate denominator, including entries that occur later. This is an exploratory result with fewer than 10 valid repeats; no uncertainty band is shown. A cross marks an unavailable result, not zero.

The physical sky-averaged LISA-selected rate is retained in `imri_rate_summary.csv` as secondary information. It is a conditional per-host yield, not a cosmological detector rate.
